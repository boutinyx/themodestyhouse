Every "best abaya brands" list I've read treats abayas like one market with a few designer outliers. They're not. I went through every abaya listed across the [directory](/modest-abayas) — 5,213 pieces, 91 houses — and priced each house by its median. The spread runs from $7 to $517. That's not a range with a few expensive edge cases in it. It's two different shopping trips.

## The everyday range

Most houses cluster here, and it's genuinely crowded — dozens of brands between $30 and roughly $230, with no clean break anywhere inside it. Four worth knowing:

**[Losyana](https://losyana.nl/products/emirate-abaya-black-1)** (Nijmegen, Netherlands) — 71 pieces, median $35. The [Emirate Abaya in black](https://losyana.nl/products/emirate-abaya-black-1) is the house at its plainest: closed-front, no embellishment, the kind of thing you buy three of.

**[Bayt El Hayat](https://baytelhayat.co.uk)** (UK) — 76 pieces, median $50. The [Beige Ribbed Abaya](https://baytelhayat.co.uk/products/beige-ribbed-abaya) is a plainer, more considered version of the same everyday brief.

**[Nour Al Houda / BNAH](https://nouralhouda.com.au)** (Sydney) — 129 pieces, median $56. The [Crinkle Batwing Abaya in moss](https://nouralhouda.com.au/products/crinkle-batwing-abaya-moss) is a genuinely different silhouette at this price point — the batwing sleeve isn't a budget-house standard.

**[Beyza](https://beyzaonline.com)** (Istanbul) — 126 pieces, median $57. Their [caped abaya](https://beyzaonline.com/products/9556-pelerinli-ferace) is the one piece here that reads more structured than its price.

## Where it gets more particular

Same market, further up the same curve — nothing separates this band from the one above it, but the tailoring, the fabric weight and the styling ambition start to change.

**[Aab](https://us.aabcollection.com)** (London) — 271 pieces, median $94. The [Cascade Kaftan in black](https://us.aabcollection.com/products/cascade-kaftan-black-ss25) is closer to eveningwear than to a daily abaya.

**[AbayaButh](https://abayabuth.com)** (UK) — the second-largest catalogue on the site, 564 pieces, median $95. Their [frill-detailed khimar set](https://abayabuth.com/products/abaya-with-frill-detailed-khimar-set-black) is a full coordinated look, not a single garment.

**[Rutba Fashion Abaya](https://rutbafashionabaya.com)** (Dubai) — 253 pieces, median $122. The [leaf-embroidery, stone-work abaya](https://rutbafashionabaya.com/products/r279-luxury-abaya-leaf-embroidery-stone-work) is embellishment-forward in a way the under-$60 band mostly isn't.

**[Kamin](https://kamin.ae)** (Dubai) — 292 pieces, median $169. Contemporary tailoring is the whole pitch; [The Maha](https://kamin.ae/products/the-maha-brown) is a clean, structured cut with none of the embellishment Rutba leans on.

**[Aeon Abaya](https://aeonabaya.net)** (Kuwait City) — 413 pieces, median $194, the top of this band. The [open-front bisht abaya](https://aeonabaya.net/products/568-open-front-bisht-abaya) borrows from menswear's most formal layer and reworks it.

## The actual gap

Here's the one hard number in this piece: sort all 91 houses by median price and there is a real, empty gap between $231 and $295 — sixty-four dollars with not a single house's median inside it. Everything below that line is one continuous market. Everything above it is a different one — seven houses, all clustered from $295 to $517, several of them (CHI-KA, Illi Studio, Almotahajiba) among the highest-volume abaya houses on the site, so this isn't a handful of one-off boutique pieces padding out a top shelf. It's a real, separately-supplied tier.

## The atelier tier

**[Latifi](https://latifi.ae)** (Dubai) — 18 pieces, median $295, sitting right at the line. Set-designed and occasion-first; the [Butterfly kaftan](https://latifi.ae/products/butterfly-kaftan) is built for an event, not a Tuesday.

**[Cult Abaya](https://cultabaya.com)** (Dubai) — 25 pieces, median $381. Campaign photography, zero florals in the whole catalogue. Their [Black Abaya](https://cultabaya.com/products/black-abaya) is deliberately the plainest thing they sell, which at this price is its own kind of statement.

**[Illi Studio](https://illistudio.com)** (Dubai) — 141 pieces, median $395, and one of the biggest abaya houses in the directory by volume despite the price. [ELAINE, in brown and teal](https://illistudio.com/products/elaine-in-brown), is colour-blocked in a way nothing in the lower bands attempts.

**[Almotahajiba](https://www.almotahajiba.com)** (Doha) — 55 pieces, median $467. One of the oldest, most established names in the directory's abaya category; [Ivory Edge](https://www.almotahajiba.com/products/5012a5466sb1) is closer to formalwear than to daywear.

**[CHI-KA](https://chikacollection.com)** (Dubai) — 195 pieces, median $517, the highest in the category — and, like Illi Studio, high-volume rather than a tiny capsule. The [striped kaftan in white](https://chikacollection.com/products/loose-stripe-kaftan-in-white) is the most minimal piece at this price point in the whole roundup.

---

If you're shopping under $200, you're in one enormous, continuous market — price alone won't tell you much about a house's style, and the difference between a $95 abaya and a $190 one is mostly tailoring and finish, not exclusivity. Above $295, you're somewhere else entirely, and the gap in between is real, not a rounding artefact of how I split the data. Browse the full range in the [abaya directory](/modest-abayas).
