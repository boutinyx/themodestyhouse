Priced every real dress in the [directory](/modest-dresses) by house median, converted to USD — 2,253 of them once underslips and layering pieces tagged the same way are out. 65 houses sell at least five; that's who's below.

## Under $50

**[HUM Clothing](/designers/hum)** (India, $18.78) is the cheapest house median in the category. Also here: **[Mukistore](/designers/mukistore)** ($27), **[The Golden Dune](/designers/golden-dune)** and **[Chic & Modesty](/designers/chic-modesty)** (~$41), and **[Modest & Timeless](/designers/modest-timeless)** ($45.56 — the same house whose hijabs are the cheapest in that category too).

## $50 to $100 — most of the market

Over thirty houses live here. **[Modesty in Style](/designers/modesty-in-style)** (Australia, $67) has the biggest catalogue in the band. **[Aab](/designers/aab)** (London, $78) and **[Veiled](/designers/veiled)** (USA, $92) are two of the directory's five sealed houses — Veiled the broadest wardrobe of any house here. **[Nihan](/designers/nihan)** and **[Touché Privé](/designers/touche-prive)** (both Turkey, ~$95–100) both build the dress around a belted waist.

## $100 to $195 — more occasion, less everyday

**[MERRACHI](/designers/merrachi)** (Amsterdam, $107) has the single largest dress catalogue in the index. **[Glow Modesty](/designers/glow-modesty)** ($145, sealed) is a genuine dress specialist. **[Inayah](/designers/inayah)** ($149, sealed) is the smallest of the five sealed houses; **[Summer Evenings](/designers/summer-evenings)** ($195, sealed) is the priciest — full-length, heavily worked [gowns](https://www.summerevenings.us/products/plum-embellished-chiffon-cape-gown).

## The gap, then the atelier tier

There's a real, empty stretch from $195 to $272 — the biggest jump anywhere on the list (two houses with under five dresses sit inside it, which is exactly why the cutoff is five). Above it, four houses only: **[Cult Abaya](/designers/cult-abaya)** ($272), **[CHI-KA](/designers/chi-ka)** ($327 — also the priciest house in the directory on abayas), **[BAQA](/designers/baqa)** ($364), **[Beyond Label](/designers/beyond-label)** ($408, the highest dress median here).

## Fabric moves the price a little; the house moves it a lot

Silk is the outlier at $176. Everything else — satin, linen, cotton, jersey, chiffon — sits within $39 of each other, $71 to $110. Which house you buy from swings the price by hundreds. Which fabric it's cut from swings it by tens.

Prices read from each house's own listings; shops move fast, so treat this as the shape of the market, not a quote. Same treatment exists for [abayas](/editorial/best-abaya-brands-price-tiers) and [hijabs](/editorial/where-to-buy-hijabs-online).
