Where the content comes from. Brand names, cities, categories, currencies, aesthetic tags and product counts are read from data/brands.ts and data/products.json. Nothing here is invented copy — the only text I wrote is the one-line note under each plate title, describing what that direction optimises for.

The garment images are the Style-It cutouts, standing in for brand photography. A real build would use each house's own imagery; the colour fields behind them are derived from the house tokens.

The plates stay light whichever theme you are in — they depict a light site, so rendering them dark would show you a page that does not exist. The surrounding study follows your system theme.
