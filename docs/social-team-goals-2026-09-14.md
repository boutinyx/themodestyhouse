# Social media team — goals, 15 Sep → 14 Dec 2026
**Date:** 2026-09-14 · **Status:** draft for Tina to confirm or change the numbers

## Why social matters for money
Social → a visit to themodestyhouse.com → a click out to a brand → a sale → commission.
Social only earns when it sends people **to the site**. Likes and followers do not pay.

**Never a goal: follower count.** The Instagram account was bought (138K followers, median post
reach 0.12% of them). Follower numbers measure nothing about this business.

## 3. Weekly output (what makes the goals move)
- **3 reels a week**, posted to Instagram and TikTok. Reels reach strangers (66–76% of views from
  non-followers); carousels reach almost only the bought followers (1–3%).
- **5 pins a day**, every one with a link to the matching page on the site.
- **1 collab post a week** with a brand that is listed on the site.

## 4. Rules that make the numbers readable
- **Every link carries a UTM tag**, or Pulse cannot say which channel sent the visit. Bio link:
  `?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=bio`. Pins: `utm_source=pinterest`.
  TikTok: `utm_source=tiktok`.
- **Mark paid or gifted brand content as an ad.**
- **Say so when an image is AI-generated.** The disclosure line is still open for Tina to set.
- Visuals follow the brand book (`docs/brand/brandbook.md`): aubergine, parchment, Bodoni.
