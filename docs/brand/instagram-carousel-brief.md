# The Modesty House — Instagram carousel brief

Self-contained. Paste this whole file into whatever tool is making the slides,
and attach `logo.png` alongside it. Nothing here needs the website or the repo.

Everything in sections 1–4 comes from The Modesty House brand book (Volume One)
and is not open to interpretation. Sections 5–7 are the carousel translation:
sensible starting values, adjust if a layout needs it, but keep the rules in 8.

---

## 1. What the brand is

**A curator, not a catalogue.** The Modesty House earns trust by having taste.
The brand frames the fashion and steps back; the seal does the endorsing.

Three rules, in the brand book's own words:

1. **Fashion leads.** Chrome stays quiet. Aubergine and parchment frame the
   work, never compete with it.
2. **Brass means endorsed.** Gold is reserved for moments of curation —
   Verified, Featured, Editor's Pick. Never decoration.
3. **Warm, never cold.** Welcoming and elegant. Aspirational without being
   exclusive or clinical.

Line: *"Where modest fashion is found."*
Descriptor: THE MODEST FASHION DIRECTORY.

## 2. Colour

| | hex | use |
|---|---|---|
| Aubergine | `#441943` | primary. Backgrounds for reverse slides, headlines on parchment |
| Parchment | `#F3EEE4` | the ground. Most slides are this colour |
| Antique Brass | `#A98A5B` | **endorsement only** — a seal, a "Verified" pill, a rule. Never body text, never a button, never a background |
| Ink | `#241B24` | body text |
| Bone | `#FBFAF6` | cards and panels sitting on parchment |
| Muted | `#8A7D6B` | small labels and captions. Not body text |

**Proportion across the whole carousel: parchment 60% · ink 25% · neutral 8% ·
brass a sliver.** If a slide reads as "a purple slide" or "a gold slide", it is
wrong. Aubergine is allowed to own a whole slide occasionally — a cover, a
closing slide — not most of them.

## 3. Type

| role | font | use |
|---|---|---|
| Display | **Bodoni Moda** | headlines and the wordmark. 24px and up only — it falls apart small |
| Labels | **Marcellus** | eyebrows, numbers, badges, section labels. **Always tracked caps** (letter-spacing ~0.16em) |
| Text & UI | **Jost** | body copy, captions, everything functional |

All three are free on Google Fonts. Never substitute. Never use heavy weights —
the elegance comes from thin type and whitespace, never from bold.

## 4. The mark

- One colour, always. **No gradients, ever.**
- Four approved treatments: aubergine on parchment (primary), parchment on
  aubergine (reverse), aubergine on bone (stationery), parchment on brass (seal).
- Clear space on all sides equals the height of one of the small side stars.
- It shrinks in steps: full mark with the dotted ring above 40px, the plain oval
  at 24–40px, the bare monogram below 24px.

**Never redraw, trace, restyle or regenerate the mark. Place `logo.png`.**
An AI asked to "draw the TH monogram" will invent a different one on every
slide, and a wrong logo is worse than no logo.

## 5. Canvas

- **1080 × 1350px, 4:5 portrait.** Every slide the same size — Instagram takes
  the ratio from the first slide and crops the rest to match.
- Margin: **90px on all four sides.** Nothing but a full-bleed photograph
  crosses it.
- **The cover is centre-cropped in the profile grid.** Keep the headline and
  anything that must survive inside the middle square — roughly y 135–1215 —
  or it disappears from the grid.
- Up to 20 slides; 6–10 reads best.
- Export PNG for slides that are mostly type, JPEG at high quality for slides
  that are mostly photograph.

## 6. Slide anatomy

**Cover.** One idea. Eyebrow in Marcellus tracked caps (~26px, muted or brass),
headline in Bodoni Moda (~80–96px, ink on parchment or parchment on aubergine),
and nothing else. The mark small in a corner, or omitted — it repeats on the end
slide anyway.

**Body slides.** Eyebrow + one short headline + at most three lines of Jost, or
one photograph with a single caption. Generous whitespace. A slide with two ideas
on it is two slides.

**Product slides.** The photograph on bone or white, never on aubergine or brass,
never on a colour that competes with the clothes. Brand name in Marcellus tracked
caps, piece and price in Jost. That order.

**End slide.** Aubergine ground, the mark reversed in parchment, *"Where modest
fashion is found."* in Bodoni Moda italic, then THE MODESTY HOUSE in tracked caps
and `themodestyhouse.com` small in brass.

## 7. Photography

The clothes are the subject and the brand is the frame. Warm, natural light.
Neutral or bone backgrounds. No heavy filters, no cool blue grades, no busy
props. If a photograph and the layout are competing, the layout loses.

## 8. Non-negotiables

**Do**
- Keep parchment as the ground on most slides.
- Use brass only where something is genuinely being endorsed.
- Leave more space than feels necessary.
- Place the real logo file; use the real three fonts.

**Don't**
- Don't put a gradient on anything, especially the mark.
- Don't set body text in Bodoni, or headlines in Jost.
- Don't use bold weights or title case in labels — labels are tracked caps.
- Don't colour a background behind a product photograph.
- Don't let AI render the wordmark or headline text as part of a generated
  image: image models garble letterforms. **Generate the imagery, then set the
  type over it** in Canva, Figma or similar, with the real fonts.
- Don't invent claims about a brand — prices, materials, provenance, "the best".
  Copy is written by Tina.
