## Why — read off the actual SERPs, not inferred

**`merrachi`** (619 impressions, 0 clicks). What sits above us on google.nl:

```
1  MERRACHI  merrachi.com   + five sitelinks: Hijabs · Dresses · Scarves · Sets · Shop all
2  MERRACHI (@merrachi) · Instagram
3  MERRACHI          4  merrachi
5  Merrachi reviews — Trustpilot     6  Merrachi — Vinted     7  Marktplaats
   plus a knowledge panel down the right
```

Someone typing a brand's name wants that brand, and gets it in the first result with a
six-link menu. A directory page at position 7 is below the fold *and* answering a question
that was already answered. **No title, description or schema fixes that.**

**`diversity modest`** (187 impressions, **12 clicks, 5.3%**). We are the **third domain**
on the page — and their own site's sitelinks read *"This collection is empty"* and
*"Something Comfy is coming"*. Their storefront is thin, so our page — 42 pieces, with
prices — is genuinely the more useful result.

That is the whole pattern, and it is repeatable: **our designer page wins exactly where the
brand's own web presence is weak.** The eight that earn clicks are small or Instagram-first
houses (diversity-modest 5.3%, fares 5.6%, lafemme 3.8%, avyaana 3.8%, chic-modesty 2.8%).
The fifteen that earn nothing are houses with a real storefront of their own.

Also observed: Google **rewrites our titles** on these anyway — the SERP shows
*"Diversity Modest — Modest Fashion Brand - The Modesty House"*, which is not the title the
page serves.

## What the honest opportunity is

**1. Impressions are the wrong number to watch.** They grew 6.5x and 65% of the growth is
on queries that cannot convert. Clicks — 40 in 28 days — is the number. Reporting the
impressions curve as progress, which the 2026-09-02 log did, oversold it.

**2. The winnable designer pages are identifiable, and there are ~66 unexamined.** The
property that predicts a click is *the brand's own site being weak or absent*. That is
measurable per brand and nobody has measured it.

**3. The lanes are the real prize and they are nowhere.** 26 lane pages, **917 impressions,
6 clicks**, positions 47–65. That is where non-brand intent lives and where a directory can
legitimately beat a shop. It needs authority, not markup.

**4. Editorial is the only non-brand thing that converts.**
`/editorial/best-abaya-brands-price-tiers` runs 5.4% CTR at position 9.6.
`/editorial/where-to-buy-hijabs-online` has 77 impressions at position 36.8 and no clicks.
Four posts exist.

**5. `/directory` still draws 166 impressions at position 49** on a URL that 308s to
`/new-in`. Google has not migrated the equity yet. Worth watching, not acting on.

## The proportion that matters most

Last 30 days by source: **Instagram ~130 visitors · ChatGPT ~102 · Google ~51.** Google
organic is the smallest of the three, and 40 clicks in 28 days is the ceiling of everything
described above. Any judgement about where to spend effort next should start there rather
than from the impressions chart.
