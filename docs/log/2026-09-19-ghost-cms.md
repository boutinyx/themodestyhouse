   Members and drafts remain un-backed-up (the spec's open item). This is a second direct-to-`main` data
   writer besides `refresh.yml`; CLAUDE.md §1 names only the first as an exception.
5. **Railway health check:** Ghost answers Railway's plain-http probe with a 301 to its https `url`, so the
   deploy stayed `DEPLOYING` for >5 minutes though Ghost booted in 20 s, and Railway would not route the custom
   domain to it. Cleared → `SUCCESS` in 9 s. `serviceInstanceUpdate(healthcheckPath: null)` is silently
   ignored; only `""` clears it. Railway also needs a `_railway-verify.<host>` TXT before it issues a cert.
6. **Copy for Tina:** the pill still says "Thank you — you're on the list" the moment a reader submits, but

   safe locally and in git.
