# Instagram launch order: research and agent debate
**Date:** 2026-10-15 · **Status:** done

## Goal
Tina asked for deep research, and a debate between agents, on what the new account should post first and in what order. She suspects a new account's first post gets a boost.

## What changed
I ran a 9-agent workflow (wf_1fd3dd0f-9e0):
- algorithm research
- precedent research
- 2 agents that watched every ready video (frames plus transcription)
- 3 strategist proposals: reach, identity and test-and-learn
- a critic
- a synthesis

Outputs are in Tina's vault, `~/ModestDirectory/Instagram/`:
- `Launch order - what to post first.md`: the final recommendation
- `Launch order - research appendix.md`: the raw research, the proposals and the critique

## Key findings
- There is no evidence for a "first-post bonus". There is evidence that every post is first tested on non-followers, and on a 0-follower account every viewer is a non-follower.
- Trial Reels need at least 200 followers, so the calendar's Trial Reel on Sat 17 can't happen.
- The recommended first post is abaya-tiers (after trimming its opening), then LOOK 001, then the Birkin video.
- Hold back for now: lavers-law (factual problem, and her lips can be made out through the blur), first-hijab, the burkini doc and hot-in-that (seasonal), and kohl.
- Fix before posting: europe-hair (a Holy Week hood shot reads as KKK) and doc-abaya-colour (69 s, too long).
- Blocking, before any studio video goes out: run a face-blur check on every frame (§10.63). The studio videos have so far only been checked on samples.
