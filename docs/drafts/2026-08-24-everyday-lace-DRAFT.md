Lace searches peak in February and March, every year, going back five years. That's the run-up to Ramadan and Eid, and it tells you exactly how most people think about lace: it's for the good occasion, the one photo, the once-a-year dress.

Then I counted what's actually being made. There are **434 lace pieces from 51 houses** in this directory. **Fourteen of them** — 3.2% — have a word like *gown*, *evening*, *bridal* or *formal* anywhere in the name.

Nearly nine times as many are described as **trim**, **detail**, **panel** or **edge**. Lace isn't the dress. It's the edge of the scarf you wear on a Tuesday.

> **YOUR BIT — the opening.** Do you wear lace on an ordinary day, and what changed your mind about it if you didn't used to? This is the part that makes the data mean something.

## What the brands are actually making

The garment mix is the giveaway. If lace were occasionwear you'd expect dresses to dominate. They don't:

- **Abayas — 159 pieces (37%)**
- Dresses — 94 (22%)
- Tops — 73 (17%)
- **Hijabs — 44 (10%)**
- Skirts — 38 (9%)
- Trousers — 14 (3%)

Abayas and hijabs together are nearly half of it. Those are the two things in a modest wardrobe you put on *without thinking* — the throw-on and the everyday scarf. That's not a fabric being saved for best.

## The lace hijab is its own category now

**44 lace hijabs from 11 houses.** Not one of them is an occasion piece.

**[Vela Scarves](/directory?q=vela)** does it as a colourway — *Jasmine White Lace*, *Black Swan Lace*, *Powder Blue Lace*, $30 each, sitting in the range beside their plain ones as if lace were just another finish. **[Hawaa Clothing](/directory?q=hawaa)** puts polka modal and lace together at £28. **[Urban Modesty](/directory?q=urban%20modesty)** does a navy lace-trim modal at $24.99. **[Niswa Fashion](/directory?q=niswa)**'s *Lace Modal* comes in *Lumière* and *Swan Lake*.

Naming a lace scarf after a ballet and then pricing it at $18 tells you what they think it's for.

## The rest of the fabric story

Lace is the loudest, but it isn't alone — every fast-growing search in modest fashion this year is a **material**, not a shape:

| | growth | in the directory |
|---|---|---|
| Pashmina / cashmere | **+1,400%** | 19 pieces — the biggest gap on the site |
| Premium chiffon | +1,300% | 986 |
| Liquid jersey (UK) | +950% | 81 |
| **Lace** | **+600% UK, +400% US** | **434** |
| Embroidered | +200% | 403 |
| Velvet | +170% | 79 |

Two honest notes on that table. These are the fastest *growing* searches, not the biggest — `lace abaya` is still about 500 times smaller than plain `abaya`. And **pashmina is the one to watch**: the fastest-growing term in modest fashion anywhere, and there are nineteen pieces for it.
