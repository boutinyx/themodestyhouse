## 3. Three things measurably wrong, fixable this week

### 3.1 Seven of nine posts use a misspelled hashtag

The captions carry **`#modestfashıon`** — with a **Turkish dotless ı (U+0131)**, not the
Latin `i`. It is a different hashtag. Those posts have never appeared in the real
`#modestfashion` feed.

```
#modestfashıon  (dotless ı)  7 posts
#modestfashion  (correct)    1 post
```

Verified character by character, not by eye. This is free reach being thrown away.

### 3.2 Reels reach 2.9× carousels — on your own data

```
Reels     median reach 454   (n=4)
Carousels median reach 157   (n=5)
```

This replicates the @mdst_market finding from 2026-08-28 exactly

Two accounts, different sizes, same result. The post that says what you are *for*
outperforms the post that says *look at these clothes*.

**Also: nothing you post asks a question.** Nine declarative captions, zero comments. The
two are related.

## Next steps, in the order they should happen

### Step 1 — decide what the bought account is FOR *(settled; the question is now strategy)*

The followers are bought, so the 0.12% is structural and permanent. Instagram tests a post
on a slice of followers and expands on their engagement; that slice will never engage, so
no caption fixes it. **Follower count is not a metric for this account and never will be.**

Three honest options, with the trade-off that now matters:

**A. Keep it and lean entirely on Reels.** Reels distribution is driven substantially by
NON-followers, which is why the four reels reached 131–589 while carousels sat at 27–425.
This is the only route where the dead audience matters less. Slow, but it costs nothing and
keeps the shop.

**B. Start a clean account.** A 500-follower account at 5% engagement out-distributes this
one, and Instagram reads the follower:engagement ratio against you. **But the cost is now
concrete and was not, a week ago:** the Instagram Shop, the Meta catalogue (19,102 items),
the verified domain and the product tagging are all attached to `@themodestyhouse.hq`. A
new account means redoing that setup, and re-earning shop approval.

**C. Both — this one as a billboard, a new one as the real thing.** 139K is still useful
social proof when approaching brands to join the directory, which is a real business use
that does not depend on engagement at all.

**Recommendation: A now, C later.** Do not throw away the shop that was just built to chase
a number that was never real. Run reels-first here for 4–6 weeks and judge it on Pulse
campaign traffic, not on likes. If reach stays under ~500 with genuinely good reels, start
the clean account then — with the shop playbook already written down and repeatable.

### Step 2 — fix the hashtag *(ten minutes)*
Edit the seven captions. `#modestfashıon` → `#modestfashion`. Check your keyboard's
autocorrect, since a Turkish dotless ı does not appear by accident on a Dutch layout.

### Step 3 — change the content mix *(next ten posts)*
- **Reels over carousels** where the choice exists. 2.9× on your own data.
- **Lead with the problem, not the product.** Your three positioning posts are your three
  best. The one that named what TMH is for produced your only follow.
- **Ask one question per caption.** Zero comments across nine posts is not an audience
  problem alone; nothing invites a reply.
- **Keep the flat-lays** — they are good, and they are what the product-link work makes
  shoppable. Just stop making them the whole feed.

### Step 4 — use what was built this week *(ongoing)*
- Product tags now work: the Instagram shop is live and the catalogue holds 19,102 items.
- Every product link carries `utm_source=instagram / utm_medium=product_tag /
  utm_campaign=meta_shop`, so **Pulse → Acquisition → Campaign** will show which posts
  actually send traffic. That is the real scoreboard — likes are not.
- Tell me before featuring a product so it can be pinned past the size floor.

### Step 5 — worth doing, not urgent
- The nine posts older than June were not analysed; they pre-date this strategy and are
  spread over three years. A baseline from them would be nice, not decisive.
- **Collaborator posts.** @mdst_market's coauthored posts median 77 likes against 31 solo,
  and only 8 of 138 posts used the feature — the cheapest unused lever found in that
  analysis, and it is unused here too.
